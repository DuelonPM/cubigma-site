Frequently asked questions

I reinstalled the game and lost my progress

The game has no accounts, so progress belongs to the installation on that phone. After deleting the game or moving to a new phone, the game starts from the beginning.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the shop in the game and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

My daily reward was not counted

Daily check-ins are counted when the phone is online. A new day starts at 00:00 UTC. If a whole day is missed, an unfinished streak starts over.

How do I switch between Kid and Adult mode?

The mode is chosen when you enter the game. If you cannot switch, write to us.

How do I delete my data?

Email support with the player code from Settings. See the Privacy Policy for details.
